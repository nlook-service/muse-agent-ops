# Article Pipeline v1

The order for producing an article. A draft that skips this order goes back before scoring.

## 1. Title first
- The title is the most important part of the piece
- The title is the hook: why would anyone want to read this?
- The title must promise knowledge — the reader should feel what they will learn
- Produce 8–10 candidates, score them, show the top 3 with a recommendation

## 2. Source gathering — 5+ sources
- Search multiple relevant materials. Minimum 5 sources
- Vary the sources: reports, press articles, survey data, on-the-ground cases
- Never use a number or case you cannot source. Mark estimates as estimates

## 3. Clarify your own opinion — the most important step
- Gathered materials will hold diverse opinions. From them, clarify **what your opinion is**
- **What is the conclusion, what am I trying to say** — this matters most
- Be decisive: not "it seems that…", but "the direction is this"
- Separate fact from opinion so the reader can tell which is which
- Never fabricate first-person experiences (opinions and judgments are fine, invented anecdotes are not)

## 4. Structure — magazine-style layout
- Fix the outline (subheads) first. Subheads alone should reveal what the piece argues
- Skeleton: observational opening → standard first → one key sentence per scene → drawbacks without warning → plain closing
- Card-manuscript structure (`## subhead` + one paragraph + image) so it becomes cards and threads as-is
- 2–4 sentences per paragraph (mobile rhythm)

## 5. Photos — do they match the topic?
- Photo selection is half the content, not decoration
- First principle: beautiful or curious
- Do not illustrate the topic literally; evoke the situation or create curiosity
- Avoid stock-model faces, phone-in-hand shots, UI mockups

## 6. Insight check
- What insight does this piece give — sayable in one sentence
- Clarity of judgment: does the author take a clear stand?
- Actionability: does the reader know what to do Monday morning?

## 7. Scoring — 5-axis rubric
- `references/eval-rubric.md`: substance, hook, fun, intellectual delight, insight — 50 points
- 35/50 or above = PASS, below = REWORK
- **REWORK means rewriting the piece AND reselecting images** — a below-gate draft is not fixed with sentence edits. Rewrite targeting the weakest axis and reselect images, including the cover, from scratch
- Source list at the bottom is required (REWORK if missing)

## 8. Publish
- Publish only after the user's confirmation (check tags and visibility)
- Log with the evolve loop; when the user reacts, record the reward in the same turn
